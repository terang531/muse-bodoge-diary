[[ワークショップ設計所]]を主宰している。仕事は、話し合いや学びの場の設計（ファシリテーション）だ。誰をどんな言葉でその場へ誘うか。何を話すか。どんな時間を過ごしてもらうか。それを用意する。そこにいる人が「自分も参加している」と感じられる場を、広く「ワークショップ」と呼んでいるね。現代的なリーダーシップを学ぶ個人指導も行っている。

東京都新宿区にある空葉堂書店の運営にも関わっている。選書を担当する一人だ。一冊を三か月かけて読み進める読書会「じっくり読書会」も主宰しているね。

===EN===

terang runs [[ワークショップ設計所]], a practice for designing workshops and facilitated gatherings. Who to invite. In what words. What to talk about. How the time is spent. He prepares all of that, so that everyone present can feel they are truly participating. He also offers individual coaching on contemporary leadership.

@card wsplan

He is involved in running Kuyodo Shoten, a bookshop in Shinjuku, Tokyo. He is one of the members responsible for selecting its stock. He also hosts a slow reading group. The group spends three months on a single book.

@card kuyodo

He also designs board games: [[雅々]] (2021), Silentalk (2024), and Botetsu no Kami (2025). On Silentalk, he presented a paper, [Implementing Spontaneous Team Facilitation through Board Games: A Case Study of Silentalk](https://doi.org/10.11363/kaihatsukogaku.45.33), at a 2025 conference of the Japan Society of Developmental Engineering.

He studied complex systems at Kwansei Gakuin University. He worked in corporate sales at a steel trading company. He spent years at an organizational consulting firm. There, he developed and taught executive training programs. Then he led its turnaround as a director and president. He founded Resekkei Inc. in 2018. He also worked in human resources for the Tokyo 2020 Olympic and Paralympic organizing committee.

He writes on Cosense ([Komojis no Soshiki Kaihatsu](https://scrapbox.io/komoji-od/)) and in the note magazine Labor Hack.

Media appearances include a Business Insider / Life Insider feature on Kuyodo Shoten (2024). A fuller list is on the workshop's [media and publications page](https://ws-plan.pro/publications/).
