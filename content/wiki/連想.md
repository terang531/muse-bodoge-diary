---
title: 連想
type: mechanic
title_en: Association
title_alt: Association
---

絵や言葉から思い浮かべるイメージを手がかりにする仕組み。出す側と受ける側の感じ方の重なり具合が、そのまま遊びになる。

===EN===

A mechanism built on the images that pictures and words bring to mind. How far the giver's and the receivers' associations overlap is itself the game.
