HITSTER: Anime Songs

Year2026
PublisherGP Co., Ltd.
Players2–10 players
Playing time30 min–
AgesAges 16+
MechanicsSet Collection

It is the anime-song edition of the music game HITSTER. Songs are played through an app with Spotify. You guess the year each song was adopted as an anime song. You slot it into the right place in your timeline. The first player to collect ten cards wins. It includes 308 songs. Showa-era classics are in it. Recent hits are also in it. The original HITSTER was born in the Netherlands in 2022. It became a worldwide hit.
