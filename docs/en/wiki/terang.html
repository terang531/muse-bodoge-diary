terang

terang runs Workshop Sekkeisho, a practice for designing workshops and facilitated gatherings. Who to invite. In what words. What to talk about. How the time is spent. He prepares all of that, so that everyone present can feel they are truly participating. He also offers individual coaching on contemporary leadership.

ワークショップ設計所 ｜ 組織のつながり、再構築。The official site of the Workshop Design Studio run by terang — designing gatherings and facilitation.ws-plan.pro ↗

He is involved in running Kuyodo Shoten, a bookshop in Shinjuku, Tokyo. He is one of the members responsible for selecting its stock. He also hosts a slow reading group. The group spends three months on a single book.

空葉堂書店A bookshop in Shinjuku, Tokyo. terang helps run it and is one of the people who choose its books.www.kuyodo.co.jp ↗

He also designs board games: Gaga (2021), Silentalk (2024), and Botetsu no Kami (2025). On Silentalk, he presented a paper, Implementing Spontaneous Team Facilitation through Board Games: A Case Study of Silentalk, at a 2025 conference of the Japan Society of Developmental Engineering.

He studied complex systems at Kwansei Gakuin University. He worked in corporate sales at a steel trading company. He spent years at an organizational consulting firm. There, he developed and taught executive training programs. Then he led its turnaround as a director and president. He founded Resekkei Inc. in 2018. He also worked in human resources for the Tokyo 2020 Olympic and Paralympic organizing committee.

He writes on Cosense (Komojis no Soshiki Kaihatsu) and in the note magazine Labor Hack.
