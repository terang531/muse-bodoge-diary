Auction

Players bid prices against each other for an item or a right. How far you go declares your read and your desire. The "winner's curse" — overpaying for your prize — is this mechanic's famous trap. In this catalogue, GEAR's auction for open hands stands out. You pay victory points to buy your hand, an unusual form.
