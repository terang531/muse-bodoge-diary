Oregon

Year2007
DesignerHenrik Berg, Åse Berg
PublisherHans im Glück
Players2–4 players
Playing time45 min
AgesAges 8+
MechanicsTile Placement

The setting is the American West in 1846. You place farmers and buildings in Oregon and compete for points. On your turn you play two terrain cards. Their combination names a row and a column of the board. Near that crossing you place a farmer, or you build a building. A building scores for each adjacent farmer. Here is the catch: the other players' farmers count too. A building you raise for yourself can feed an opponent's score. Coal and gold tiles come from mines. The actions are simple, but the fight over placement is rich. A mid-weight euro by the Norwegian Berg couple.
