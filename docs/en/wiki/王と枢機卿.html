Kardinal & König

Year2000
DesignerMichael Schacht
PublisherGoldsieber
Players3–5 players
Playing time50–60 min
AgesAges 12+
MechanicsArea Majority, Hand Management

An area game set in medieval Europe. You play region cards to place abbeys and cardinals on the board. The game runs in two halves, each ending with a scoring. The regional scoring is distinctive. The player with the most abbeys in a region scores the region's abbey count. Second place scores the leader's count, third scores second's count — a chain. Chains of four or more abbeys along a road score too, as does holding the cardinal majority in two neighboring regions at once. All you do is play a card and place a piece. Yet the two kinds of pieces interlock into a dense fight. A masterpiece of area majority, remade as China in 2005 and as Han in 2014.
