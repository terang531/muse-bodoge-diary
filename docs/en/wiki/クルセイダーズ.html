Crusaders: Thy Will Be Done

Year2018
DesignerSeth Jaffee
PublisherTasty Minstrel Games
Players2–4 players
Playing time30–60 min
MechanicsMancala

You lead a knightly order in the Crusades, choosing actions through a Mancala mechanism: you distribute pieces across spaces, and the space where the last piece lands determines your action. Build buildings, go on campaigns, and spread your influence.
