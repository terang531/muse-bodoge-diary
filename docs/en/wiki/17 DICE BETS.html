17 DICE BETS

Year2026
DesignerLee Ju-Hwa, Giung Kim
PublisherMandoo Games
Players2–4 players
Playing time30–40 min
AgesAges 8+
MechanicsBetting

A dice game from Korea's Mandoo Games, using seventeen eight-sided dice. As in Texas Hold'em, you combine your hidden dice with the shared dice on the table to make hands, and bet chips on the combinations you think will land. The shared dice are re-rolled between betting rounds, so the odds keep shifting.
