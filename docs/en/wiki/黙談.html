Silentalk

Year2024
DesignerWorkshop Sekkeisho
PublisherKuyodo Bookstore
Players2–5 players
Playing time5–30 min
AgesAges 12+
MechanicsCooperative

A cooperative puzzle game played without voices or gestures. The only channel is passing wooden pieces to each other. Together, the players build the shape shown on a mission card. You cannot say "put that one over here." Speed, timing, rhythm, and the movement of hands carry every intention. The rule that bans conversation creates a denser communication instead. It was released in November 2024, designed by terang.
