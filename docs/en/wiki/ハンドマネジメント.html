Hand Management

The mechanic of deciding when, in what order, and how to play the cards in your hand. Timing matters more than the strength of the card itself. The card you cannot bring yourself to play, and the one you save until it rots — those judgments accumulate into the game. In this catalogue: Mombasa, China, and Combo Creatures.
